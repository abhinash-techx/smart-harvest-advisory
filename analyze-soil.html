Soil Testing Kit Guide

Use a kit as a quick field check. For important fertilizer decisions, confirm results with a reliable laboratory.

What does a kit test?

pHAcidic / neutral / alkaline condition
NNitrogen — plant growth and leaf development
PPhosphorus — roots, flowering and energy transfer
KPotassium — plant strength and stress tolerance

How to use a basic chemical kit

  1. Collect a representative soil sample from several spots and mix it well.
  2. Follow the kit manual for the exact soil quantity, water/reagent ratio and extraction time.
  3. Add the specified reagent and compare the colour/readout exactly as the manufacturer instructs.
  4. Record pH/NPK values and keep the test date with the result.
  5. If the result is unusual or a major fertilizer decision depends on it, verify with a laboratory.
